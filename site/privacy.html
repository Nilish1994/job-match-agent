Privacy

Your data, in plain language

JobPilot keeps only what it needs to find you jobs and draft applications, and you can delete all of it with one command.

What we store

  • Your profile, which Claude extracts from your CV: your experience, skills, projects, education and job preferences. It is encrypted at rest.
  • Your matches: the jobs scored for you, with their scores, reasons and gaps.
  • Application statuses: whether you approved, skipped or applied for a job, and how it went.
  • Your Telegram chat id, so the bot knows where to send your matches. We don't see your phone number.

What we don't store

  • Your CV file. It's read once to build your profile and then discarded.
  • We never sell your data, show you ads or share your details with employers or recruiters. Nothing is sent to an employer unless you send it yourself.

Who else sees it Claude API

To build your profile, score jobs and draft your CV, cover letter and answers, your CV text and profile are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic processes them to return a response, under its commercial terms, which say it doesn't train models on API data by default.

Messages and files travel through Telegram, under Telegram's privacy policy. Job listings come from Adzuna; none of your data is sent to Adzuna.

Deleting your data

Send /deleteme to the bot at any time. It removes everything about you: your profile, matches, application statuses and chat id. It can't be undone, and you're welcome to request a new invite later.

Questions

Message the JobPilot bot on Telegram and you'll get a reply there.
